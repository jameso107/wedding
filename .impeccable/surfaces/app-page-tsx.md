# Surface: Save-the-date (`/`)

Mode: Persuade
Audience: invited guests on a phone, opening oosterhouse.wedding from a link or QR
Job: lock Sunday, July 4, 2027 at Bay Pointe Inn, Gun Lake, onto a calendar
Action: read the card, flip it, remember the date and the inn
Proof: names, date, and venue only — no couple photography on hand
Constraints: save-the-date only; no RSVP, nav, registry, or story; coastal palette is light blue and dark maroon; motion is required

## Direction contract

THESIS: The save-the-date is one Gun Lake linen postcard you can tilt and flip. It refuses the centered-names-over-a-coastal-wash wedding homepage.

OWN-WORLD: Cool fog-white linen stock (`#E7F3F7`) as the card, lake cyan (`#8EC8DC`) owning the entire face as unmixed water, dark maroon (`#6B1020`) owning only the postmark and verso ink. Linen tooth, circular date-stamp bite, and handwriting are construction, not decoration. No site chrome. Type is postal gothic on the stamp, handwritten ink on the address, never a wedding script lockup.

STORY: A guest understands James and Hannah are getting married Sunday, July 4, 2027 at Bay Pointe Inn on Gun Lake, believes this is the couple’s own card, and leaves with the date.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Edge-to-edge postcard, no header. Photo-face: full lake field, Bay Pointe Inn as a small caption that loses to the names, JAMES + HANNAH as the addressee at fighting scale, circular maroon postmark SHELBYVILLE MICH / JUL 4 2027 at the upper right. Pointer tilts the card from live values; click or tap flips it with inertia and a card-stock snap. Verso: divided postcard back, handwritten save-the-date, maroon ink, the inn and Gun Lake, Michigan written as the destination.

FORM: Linen Lake Postcard — grounded candidate 6 of 7 (mid-century Michigan linen lake postcards). Seed key `295add8e`. Raises kept: hall-scale object; one-sheet evidence; fighting scale; physical mark and snap; displacing fields.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
